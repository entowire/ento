Access

Who can reach what. Anything not allowed here is blocked.

Rules

Let reach on
Everything else is blocked, between people's devices too. One way: only the first group opens connections; the other answers. Ports and direction are enforced by the receiving device.

Device checks

Devices report their system version, disk encryption, firewall and Secure Boot when they start, when it changes and every 10 minutes. A rule that requires a check leaves out every device that fails it, or whose report is missing or too old. Phones' verified boot and patch level are what their secure hardware attests.

Groups

People groups hold people, device groups hold devices. Rules on the Access page join them.
